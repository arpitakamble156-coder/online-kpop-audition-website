ABOUT STARWAVE

Your Stage. Your Dream. Your Star.

StarWave is an online platform created to connect aspiring performers with K-pop audition opportunities.

WHO WE ARE

Helping Talents Take Their First Step

StarWave Audition is an online audition platform designed for aspiring singers, dancers, rappers and performers.

Our goal is to make the audition process simple, organized and accessible for talented people who dream of entering the K-pop industry.

Applicants can discover auditions, create their profile, submit their audition materials and track their application status in one place.
